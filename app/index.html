    Roadmap to the platinum

    Nine stages in play order, built from the official descriptions. Roughly 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough plus chapter replay for anything missed. Do the missables before their last chances pass — everything else can be cleaned up after the story.

      Missables — last chance

      Three trophies can be missed outright, and four more are at risk if you fail them badly. Everything else is replayable in free roam or by chapter replay.
